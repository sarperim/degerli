- **USER-AUTHORIZED PRODUCT CHANGE (2026-10-19/20, standing overnight-autonomy routing):** decouple the snapshot write from the P/E constituents requirement. Previously `MarketSnapshotEngine` required a balance-sheet `SHARES_DILUTED > 0` for EVERY constituent (the `continue` guard at `src/Ingestion/Metrics/MarketSnapshotEngine.cs:159-163`); with zero share data `constituents.Count == 0` → `ComputeAsync` returns null → no row → `GET /market/overview` serves `state: unavailable`, even though indices, breadth, movers and total volume are computable from stored prices alone.
- Scope: **constituents without a usable `SHARES_DILUTED` (null or ≤ 0) are no longer dropped from the snapshot — they enter the price-derived aggregates and are excluded from the market-cap-dependent ones, each carrying its honest empty state.** Concretely: (a) a constituent with today's price and a previous close participates in **breadth (FR-MOV-003), top movers + volume eligibility (FR-MOV-004/021, BR-MOV-011) and total volume (FR-MOV-005)** regardless of share data; (b) market P/E (FR-MOV-006, BR-MOV-010, D-05) is computed over share-bearing constituents with NI > 0 only — `marketPe: null` when none qualify, and `marketPeExcludedCount` counts EVERY constituent not entering the P/E sum (loss-makers, missing income AND no-share members — the exclusion is reported, never dropped); (c) market dividend yield (FR-MOV-006) is Σ DIV_TTM / Σ mcap over share-bearing constituents — `null` when there are none (TTM DPS × shares is not computable without shares; never fabricated); (d) cap-weighted sector performance (FR-MOV-002) weights only share-bearing members; a sector with no share-bearing member is **omitted from the dictionary** (an explicit `0.0` entry would be a fabricated reading, and an empty dictionary is the honest state) — in a zero-shares world `sectorPerformance` is `{}`; (e) the engine-level null return survives ONLY for its original meanings: no XU100 membership, no stored prices for the covered universe on the resolved day. The snapshot row is written (and the overview served `state: available`) whenever the price-derived blocks are computable. **The FU fixture world — where every constituent has shares — must keep byte-identical behavior** (goldens, excluded counts, sector weights, P/E values all unchanged); only the no-shares / partial-shares real-world path is new. UI: **no change required** — `MarketOverviewPage.tsx` already renders `marketPe`/`marketDivYield`/`marketPeExcludedCount` nulls with the honest `honestData.unavailableTitle` state (verified 2026-10-20, `src/Web/src/routes/MarketOverviewPage.tsx:463-491`).
