- **Where the computation lives (pinned from the architecture, verified on the current head):** `GET /stocks/{symbol}/financials` already computes per-period figures server-side from stored lines (`Line(EBITDA) ?? Sum(EBIT, DEPR_AMORT)`, `CanonicalMetrics.FreeCashFlow` — `StockFundamentalsEndpoints.cs:125-141`) and the web client computes nothing (BR-RES-004, SCR-005 §6). - **What to implement:**
  1. **Persist 090's decode-result span and 093's filing flag (additive, forward-only, no backfill):** `financial_statements` gains `period_start_date` (date NULL), `duration_months` (int NULL) — carrying `KapPublicLine.PeriodStartDate`/`DurationMonths` through `Group.ToFact()` into the store, so the payload can label interims by their declared cumulative range — and `consolidated` (bool NULL — honest absence for pre-migration rows, never guessed). The payload gains the filing-metadata envelope: `consolidated` badge input (from the flag; absent → badge suppressed, never a guessed value), **TMS 29 badge derived from `period_end_date >= 2023-12-31`** (the architecture's own pinned transition, `02` §6.2 — a derivation, never a claim about the filing's content), **KAP Kaynak badge from the served statement's `source_ref` evidence class** (`kap-public`/`kap`).
  3. **The [C] derived rows, server-side, honest-null on any missing operand** (the `Sum()`/`Line()` seam's own discipline — never a guess): per period — Revenue Growth Y/Y % (prior same-period statement, absent → null), Gross/Operating/EBITDA/Effective-Tax/Net-Margin-Parent %, Net Working Capital (`CUR_ASSETS − CUR_LIAB`, the canonical codes), Gross Debt (`ST_DEBT + LT_DEBT`, **the fenced DR-2 narrow definition — consumed as stored, never widened**), Net Debt, Tangible Book Value (equity − goodwill+intangibles, from 093's codes), BVPS / TBV-per-share / FCF-per-share (**only where a count exists**: statement `SHARES_DILUTED` first, the 086 price-derived count second — the pinned precedence; honest null/"—" otherwise, the user's exclusion #4 — never a paid-in-capital-derived guess); the EV header inputs (share price from `daily_prices`, market cap, gross debt, cash & ST investments, NCI) served as their own facts with as-of, **display-only — the DCF's own inputs are untouched**.
